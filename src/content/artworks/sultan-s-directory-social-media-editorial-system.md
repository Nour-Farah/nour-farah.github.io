An editorial carousel concept for Sultan Bakery, exploring a more character-driven approach to social media

The project aims to turn Sultan’s Directory (which is a mini Instagram series about local food culture) into a visual field guide that celebrates the people, places, and stories behind Lebanon’s food scene


---


The goal was to create a repeatable editorial system, specifically for their directory, that feels distinctly Sultan without becoming repetitive or overly commercial

What made it tough is that the layout had to be flexible enough to accommodate different locations, people, stories, quotes, and amounts of copy without every feature looking identical

Sultan’s Arabic wordmark is full of character and expression, making it a natural starting point

By turning its distinctive letterforms into graphic elements, we can build a versatile visual language

Below are three examples of how this approach comes to life

![SB1](https://res.cloudinary.com/iquk7s3z/image/upload/v1793054649/SB-01.webp)

- By elongating and flipping the Arabic letter “ن”, we can create a speech-bubble-like shape

- Enlarging the letter “ز” creates a natural space for text, making it ideal for long headlines with minimal copy

- Extending the upper part of “خ” and cropping its lower section creates a clean rectangular space at the bottom of the post for a title

These are just some of the infinite possibilities

![SB2](https://res.cloudinary.com/iquk7s3z/image/upload/v1793054649/SB-02.webp)

With the design elements established, the next step is to set a grid and some guidelines to keep every post consistent

A template, if you will

![SB3](https://res.cloudinary.com/iquk7s3z/image/upload/v1793054649/SB-03.webp)

Consistent margins on all sides, with extra space at the bottom for the logo, define the universal grid

These proportions must remain identical across every post

**Cover Slide** (Above Left)
*- Title:* Any font size, always with a drop shadow
*- Caption:* May include a background design element, keep its font size consistent with the “Sultan’s Directory Issue” label
*- Alignment:* All elements are center-aligned and can shift vertically depending on the background image/video, the logo remains fixed

**Carousel Slides** (Above Right)
*- Typography:* Headline and body text must maintain consistent font sizes across slides.
*- Headline:* Always placed within or over a design element.
*- Contrast:* Add a text shadow when needed for legibility.

After the system is laid out, we can finally see it in action!
