Visual identity and poster design for **Weigh Your Words**, a multilingual awareness campaign addressing the harmful effects of body shaming.

The project includes logo, typography, color system and three poster designs.

Posters were designed in three different languages: English, Arabic, and French.
And were displayed at NDU – Notre Dame University Louaize.
