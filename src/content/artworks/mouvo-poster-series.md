**Mouvo** is an annual international conference and festival focused on digital art, motion design, and visual communication

This conceptual poster series was created as promotional material for the 2024 Event

Inspired by the name, the poster conveys a lot of movement by expanding and contracting the main letters in certain ways to create a big curve

All three posters contain the exact same information, but with different emphasis in each one

First poster focuses on the name, second one on the location and the third on the date

Since all three posters contain the same information, they can be separated without a problem

keep them side by side and you'll get a better viewing experience
