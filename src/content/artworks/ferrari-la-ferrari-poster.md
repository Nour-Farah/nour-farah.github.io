One of the world's best cars

**The Ferrari La Ferrari**

Made into a poster series

Each poster can live on it's own, Combine all three and you get the complete set

The simple 6 x 4 grid, together with the small details (like the repeating flag of Italy and Ferrari logo) keep the designs consistent across all three posters

And most importantly, what ties the poster series together is the layout of the pictures

(This is more visible if you squint)

The positioning of the pictures, as well as the choice of picture in each position guide the viewer's eye in this wide valley curve
