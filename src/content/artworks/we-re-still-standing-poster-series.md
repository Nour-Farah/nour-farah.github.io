Lebanon has been and still is going through a lot of hardship

Despite all the problems, the Lebanese are **Still Standing** and always will be

This poster series commemorates that

In terms of design, the goal here was to create an avant-garde style poster using the same elements to convey different meaning

The following elements were chosen:

- Cedar tree, representing the people of Lebanon
- Fire extinguisher
- 500 LBP coin, reflecting the Lebanese currency as a whole
- Building
- Flame
- Smoke

All these elements work together differently in each poster

Whether it's working hard to fight the collapsing currency, or trying their best to balance all responsibilities at the same time, the Lebanese will always persist!
